# MyTrainX Evidence Standard — 2026

Status: ACTIVE
Date: 2026-09-25

## Objective
Keep consumer education current without turning MyTrainX into a diagnosis or medical-treatment product.

## Source hierarchy
Tier A — official bodies, position stands, national guidelines and canonical public datasets.
Tier B — systematic reviews, meta-analyses and high-quality evidence syntheses.
Tier C — qualified professional education and recognized textbooks.
Tier D — commercial courses, legacy materials and PLR references.

Tier D may inspire structure or topics, but does not independently validate a health claim.

## Current anchors
Physical activity: WHO guidance.
Resistance training: ACSM 2026 position stand for healthy adults.
Nutrition data: USDA FoodData Central.
Brazilian dietary education: Ministério da Saúde Guia Alimentar, subject to applicable licensing rules.

## Claim classes
- general_education
- training_guidance
- nutrition_education
- medical_sensitive
- prohibited_without_clinical_review

## Mandatory specialist review
Disease management, medications, hormones, injury diagnosis, rehabilitation prescriptions, pregnancy-specific contraindications, eating-disorder treatment and therapeutic supplement claims.

## Content aging
Every evidence-sensitive item records reviewed_at, review_due_at, evidence_version, reviewer and sources.
Suggested cadence:
- general exercise education: 24 months;
- nutrition education: 12–24 months;
- clinical/supplement-sensitive: 6–12 months;
- immediately after major authoritative guidance changes.

## AI requirement
Coach X should prefer reviewed canonical MyTrainX knowledge and must not elevate an old course or PLR claim over current reviewed evidence.
