# MyTrainX Evidence Review Policy 2026

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-09-25

## Purpose

Prevent outdated course material, marketing claims and unsourced AI text from becoming MyTrainX health/fitness guidance.

## Evidence registry

All cornerstone guidance is registered independently from user-facing content.

Each record includes:
- source organization;
- title;
- source class;
- publication/update date;
- jurisdiction/population;
- current-status check date;
- URL/identifier;
- rights notes;
- topics;
- use policy.

## Current 2026 anchors

### Resistance training
ACSM 2026 Position Stand:
`Resistance Training Prescription for Muscle Function, Hypertrophy, and Physical Performance in Healthy Adults: An Overview of Reviews`.

Use for general healthy-adult resistance-training education.

### General physical activity
WHO Guidelines on Physical Activity and Sedentary Behaviour remain a global public-health anchor.

U.S. Physical Activity Guidelines, Second Edition, remains the current U.S. edition in 2026; development of a third edition is underway for a future release.

### Nutrition
Use a jurisdiction-aware approach:
- Brazilian users: Brazilian Ministry of Health food-based guidance as a primary cultural/public-health reference;
- broader evidence: current national guidelines and systematic evidence;
- U.S. context where relevant: Dietary Guidelines for Americans 2025–2030.

### Nutrient composition
USDA FoodData Central may be used as a canonical open nutrient-data source; preserve the FDC identifier/source.

## Conflict policy

When reputable guidance differs:
1. identify population and jurisdiction;
2. distinguish minimum public-health guidance from performance programming;
3. do not silently average incompatible recommendations;
4. present uncertainty when material;
5. escalate clinical questions to a qualified professional.

## Clinical boundary

MyTrainX general content does not:
- diagnose;
- treat disease;
- prescribe medication;
- replace registered dietitians/nutritionists, physicians or physiotherapists;
- turn generic exercise guidance into a rehabilitation plan.

## Evidence refresh trigger

Mark content `update_required` when:
- a major guideline is replaced;
- a new authoritative position stand changes the practical recommendation;
- a source is withdrawn;
- a safety signal materially changes advice;
- the source URL/license materially changes.
