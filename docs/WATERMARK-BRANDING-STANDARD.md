# MyTrainX Branding & Watermark Standard

Version: 1.0
Date: 2026-09-25

## Purpose
Protect and consistently identify MyTrainX-owned or properly licensed derivative assets.

A watermark is branding, not a rights-clearing mechanism.

## Eligibility
Apply MyTrainX watermark only to:
- MyTrainX-owned assets;
- commissioned assets with assigned rights;
- PLR/derivative assets whose verified license permits modification and branding;
- open/public-domain assets where branding does not misrepresent authorship and attribution requirements are preserved.

Do not watermark third-party reference-only material as if MyTrainX created it.

## Visual forms
Primary: MYTRAINX wordmark plus optional mytrainx.fit.
Secondary: MyTrainX symbol/app mark.
Editorial footer: © MyTrainX • mytrainx.fit • version/date.

## Placement
Ebook/PDF: strong brand on cover, subtle footer internally, optional low-opacity corner watermark on premium download pages.
Exercise images: lower corner, safe margin, never cover joints or equipment.
Video: persistent small corner bug plus branded intro/outro.
Recipe images: corner mark only when rights permit.

## Watermark strength
Target recognizable but premium:
- 8–18% opacity for background/repeating watermark;
- 35–70% for small corner marks depending on image;
- full opacity for footer/logo lockups.

## Rights and attribution
Required attribution remains visible or accessible. MyTrainX branding must never imply authorship or endorsement by official bodies, studies or third-party brands.

## Export variants
For ebooks/manuals: web reading version, member PDF, print-friendly variant if relevant, social previews, cover and thumbnail.
For video: master, streaming derivative, vertical/social cut, thumbnail, transcript and captions.

## Naming
Pattern:
mytrainx_<vertical>_<product>_<asset>_v<version>_<locale>

Example:
mytrainx_nutrition_fundamentals_ebook_v1_pt-br.pdf
