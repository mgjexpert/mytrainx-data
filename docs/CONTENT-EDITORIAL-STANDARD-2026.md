# MyTrainX Editorial Standard 2026

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-09-25  
**Applies to:** articles, lessons, ebooks, manuals, recipes, workout education, scripts, captions, AI knowledge documents and downloadable products.

## Editorial mission

MyTrainX content must feel like one coherent premium performance publication: technically responsible, practical, visually disciplined and understandable by a motivated non-specialist.

We write to help a reader make a better next decision, not to impress the reader with jargon.

## Voice

### Core attributes
- precise;
- energetic without hype;
- evidence-aware;
- practical;
- human;
- inclusive;
- direct;
- premium;
- never militarized outside WKT-specific products.

### Avoid
- guaranteed transformations;
- miracle language;
- shame;
- "detox" as a physiological cure;
- "food that burns fat";
- "boost your metabolism" unless the mechanism and magnitude are accurately explained;
- demonizing foods;
- unsupported hormone manipulation;
- diagnosing pain, injury or disease;
- pretending one protocol is universal.

## Writing pattern

Preferred lesson structure:

1. **Why this matters**
2. **What to know**
3. **How to apply it**
4. **Common mistakes**
5. **Make it easier**
6. **Progress when ready**
7. **Safety / when to get help**
8. **Key takeaways**
9. **Sources / review metadata**

For short web content, compress the same logic rather than abandoning it.

## Evidence labels

Each publishable educational claim should be compatible with one of:

- `official_guideline`
- `systematic_review`
- `position_stand`
- `consensus`
- `primary_research`
- `expert_practice`
- `editorial_principle`

Do not present `expert_practice` or `editorial_principle` as established scientific fact.

## 2026 fitness baseline

For healthy adults, MyTrainX general education should be consistent with current public-health guidance and the 2026 ACSM resistance-training position stand.

Editorial consequences:
- emphasize that some activity is better than none;
- encourage gradual progression;
- treat adherence/consistency as a primary design variable;
- train major muscle groups regularly;
- distinguish strength, hypertrophy, power, conditioning, mobility and skill goals;
- do not require training to momentary failure for general results;
- do not imply that machines, free weights, bands or bodyweight are inherently superior in all contexts;
- avoid complexity for its own sake.

## 2026 nutrition baseline

Nutrition content should:
- prioritize dietary patterns and food quality over isolated "superfoods";
- distinguish education from individualized nutrition therapy;
- make protein, carbohydrate, fat, fiber and energy balance understandable without moralizing;
- support culturally appropriate foods;
- use normalized nutrient data from a provenance-tracked source;
- clearly label calculated nutrition as estimates;
- separate general healthy-population content from disease-specific nutrition.

## Localization

Primary content language: Portuguese (Brazil), with architecture prepared for PT-PT, Spanish and English.

Do not merely machine-translate exercise names. Maintain:
- canonical English/source alias;
- PT-BR preferred name;
- PT-PT preferred name when different;
- synonyms commonly used in gyms;
- anatomical/movement terminology.

## Content originality

### Owned
May use full MyTrainX branding and watermark.

### Verified PLR
Must be materially transformed before release:
- rewrite;
- fact-check;
- update;
- restructure;
- redesign;
- replace generic examples;
- add MyTrainX methodology;
- preserve license evidence.

### Public domain / compatible open data
Normalize and improve editorially while preserving provenance.

### Reference-only
May inform internal analysis, topic maps and original product planning.
Do not copy protected prose, images, video, exercise demonstrations or branded program structure in a way that substitutes for the original.

## AI-ready writing

Every long document should support later chunking:
- meaningful headings;
- one concept per section;
- explicit definitions;
- no critical context hidden only in images;
- tables accompanied by textual meaning;
- source/evidence metadata;
- safety notes kept near the relevant recommendation.

## Publication states

- draft
- editorial_review
- subject_matter_review
- legal_rights_review
- design_review
- qa
- approved
- published
- update_required
- archived

No asset becomes `published` merely because the manuscript is complete.

## Updating

Every evergreen health/fitness article should carry:
- content version;
- authored date;
- last evidence review date;
- next planned review date;
- evidence sources;
- reviewer roles.

Recommended review cadence:
- general exercise education: 12–18 months;
- nutrition guidance: 12 months or on major guideline change;
- safety/clinical-adjacent content: 6–12 months;
- rapidly changing regulatory/product content: as needed.

## MyTrainX quality test

Before approval, ask:
1. Is this useful without the marketing language?
2. Is the central claim still accurate in 2026?
3. Could a reasonable reader misinterpret it as medical advice?
4. Are rights and provenance explicit?
5. Can Coach X retrieve this safely?
6. Does the piece have a practical next action?
7. Does it look and sound like MyTrainX?
