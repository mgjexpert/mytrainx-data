# MyTrainX Content Production — Roles, AI & Human Review

## Principle

Use one editorial/architecture authority and specialized production tools. Do not create five independent AIs rewriting the same material.

## Primary editorial authority

The main MyTrainX content/architecture workflow owns:
- information architecture;
- product concepts;
- curricula;
- manuscript writing;
- rewriting and modernization;
- source comparison;
- rights metadata;
- evidence registry;
- exercise/recipe schemas;
- content manifests;
- repository documentation;
- ingestion logic;
- integration specifications;
- QA checklists.

## Specialized AI/tool support

### Image generation / image editing
Use for:
- ebook covers;
- editorial illustrations;
- recipe visual concepts;
- non-clinical diagrams;
- marketing variants;
- branded backgrounds.

Technique-critical exercise images should be reviewed against real biomechanics before publication.

### Video production AI
Use for:
- editing;
- captions;
- chaptering;
- cleanup;
- b-roll;
- animation;
- localized voice tracks where rights permit.

Do not rely on generative video alone for technique-critical movement demonstrations unless a qualified reviewer approves the movement.

### Speech/transcription
Use for:
- transcripts;
- subtitles;
- searchable video chapters;
- initial translations.

All public-facing technical terminology should be reviewed.

### Data/ETL automation
Use for:
- Drive inventory;
- PDF extraction;
- PLR license discovery;
- exercise import;
- USDA mapping;
- duplicate detection;
- metadata enrichment.

## Human approval roles

### Exercise professional
Required for:
- high-risk/advanced exercise technique;
- calisthenics progressions;
- public exercise safety cues;
- advanced programming.

### Nutrition professional
Required for:
- clinical nutrition;
- prescriptive meal plans;
- disease-specific content;
- supplement protocols beyond basic education;
- final review of nutrition products when marketed as professional guidance.

### Medical/physiotherapy review
Required for:
- injury/rehab;
- disease treatment;
- medication;
- respiratory/clinical protocols;
- pregnancy/postpartum clinical guidance when relevant.

### Legal/rights review
Required when:
- PLR terms are ambiguous;
- commercial redistribution language conflicts;
- third-party brands/assets remain;
- sublicensing/resale rights matter.

## Decision

Other general-purpose AI systems are optional, not required.

The highest-value external support is specialized production and expert validation, not duplicate generic writing.
