# MyTrainX Editorial & Visual Content System — 2026

Status: ACTIVE
Date: 2026-09-25

## Editorial promise
MyTrainX content must feel current, useful, evidence-aware, practical, premium, visually disciplined, human and free from exaggerated promises.

## Writing principles
1. Explain before prescribing.
2. Actionable over encyclopedic.
3. No miracle language or guaranteed transformation claims.
4. Distinguish established guidance, emerging evidence and expert judgment.
5. Make personalization explicit.
6. Treat safety as part of quality.
7. PT-BR is the initial canonical consumer language.
8. Write modularly so one chapter can become web content, ebook content and Coach X knowledge.

## Canonical lesson structure
- Title
- One-sentence promise
- Why it matters
- Core concept
- Practical framework
- Examples
- Common mistakes
- Safety / limitations
- Do this now
- Related content
- Sources and review metadata

## Evidence freshness
Training fundamentals should align with current authoritative guidance such as the ACSM 2026 resistance-training position stand and WHO physical-activity guidance.
Nutrition content should rely on current official dietary guidance and canonical nutrient data such as USDA FoodData Central where appropriate.

## Exercise writing
Every exercise page should contain canonical name, PT-BR name and aliases, purpose, muscles, movement pattern, equipment, level, setup, execution, breathing cue, coaching cues, common errors, regressions, progressions, alternatives, safety notes, demo media, programming examples and source/reviewer metadata.

## Recipe writing
Every recipe should contain title, description, portions, prep/cook time, normalized ingredients, steps, estimated nutrition, allergens, substitutions, storage guidance, tags, shopping-list-ready ingredients and review status.

## Ebook structure
1. Cover
2. Legal/safety notice
3. How to use
4. Table of contents
5. Foundations
6. Progressive chapters
7. Practical plans
8. Checklists/worksheets
9. FAQ
10. Sources
11. Next step in MyTrainX

Digital-first: chapters must be independently renderable in the web library.

## Design language
Inherits BLACK PERFORMANCE SYSTEM + MYTRAINX ORANGE.

- matte black / charcoal for covers and hero sections;
- white/off-white reading surfaces for long-form accessibility;
- orange as controlled accent;
- semantic green only for genuine success states;
- condensed/high-impact display type for titles;
- neutral readable sans for body;
- no generic PLR-style layouts.

## Reusable editorial components
Definition Card, Coach X Tip, Common Mistake, Progression, Regression, Safety Note, Evidence Note, Quick Checklist, Workout Card, Recipe Card, Macro Card, Equipment Card, Related Content and CTA to Coach X.

## 2026 training baseline
Current MyTrainX education should reflect:
- consistency over unnecessary complexity;
- goal-specific programming;
- progressive overload through multiple variables;
- weekly volume relevance for hypertrophy;
- heavier loading for maximal strength;
- appropriate loading and rapid concentric intent for power;
- individualization, recovery and progression;
- some activity is better than none.
