# MyTrainX Nutrition Fundamentals — V1

Status: DRAFT / ORIGINAL PRODUCTION
Format target: web course + ebook + Coach X knowledge
Review target: qualified nutrition professional

## Positioning
Education for everyday food decisions and training support. Not a disease-treatment diet.

## Modules
1. Nutrition without noise
2. Energy balance: what it means and what it does not mean
3. Protein
4. Carbohydrates
5. Dietary fats
6. Fiber and food quality
7. Hydration
8. Portions and meal construction
9. Reading labels
10. Eating around training
11. Meal planning
12. Sustainable habits
13. Supplements: evidence, limits and caution
14. Myths and marketing claims

## Editorial rules
- avoid moralizing food;
- avoid "detox" and miracle language;
- separate performance goals from medical treatment;
- distinguish general education from individualized clinical nutrition;
- use current nutrient data and reviewed references;
- make cultural and budget flexibility explicit.

## Practical tools
- balanced meal builder
- protein-source guide
- carbohydrate-source guide
- fats guide
- hydration checklist
- grocery list
- meal-prep worksheet
- pre/post training examples
- label-reading checklist
- Coach X Q&A prompts

## Data integration
Foods and recipe calculations should map to canonical food/nutrient entities, using verified open data sources where applicable.

## Output variants
- free "Nutrition Basics" mini-guide;
- full Master course;
- branded downloadable ebook;
- knowledge documents for Coach X;
- short articles derived from each module.
