# MyTrainX Kitchen — Launch Collection

Status: DRAFT / ORIGINAL PRODUCTION
Goal: structured, useful recipes integrated with nutrition data and the member experience.

## Initial target
Launch with 40 original/fully rights-cleared recipes across:
- 8 breakfasts
- 8 lunches
- 8 dinners
- 8 snacks
- 4 soups/salads
- 4 desserts/drinks

At least 12 should be practical high-protein options, but the collection should not become a single-macro diet brand.

## Recipe standard
Each recipe includes:
- portions
- prep/cook time
- ingredients
- normalized quantities
- instructions
- estimated energy/macros
- major allergens
- substitutions
- storage/reheat guidance
- equipment
- cost band later
- tags
- hero image
- MyTrainX watermark if image is owned/licensed for branding

## Product integrations
Recipe -> Kitchen page -> saved item -> shopping list -> meal-plan suggestion -> collection/ebook -> Coach X retrieval.

## Launch subcollections
- 15 Minutes
- High Protein
- Breakfast
- Lunchbox / Marmita
- Dinner
- Smart Snacks
- Meal Prep
- Budget Friendly
- Vegetarian
- Sweet without hype

## Editorial rule
Recipes can be inspired by common culinary techniques, but copied third-party recipe prose or photography should not be republished without rights.
